Title: Rubric Check

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable whole-document rubric calibration workflow. A designer submits a versioned low-stakes rubric, GenLayer validators label it CLEAR, OVERLAP, or GAPS with a closed issue code, and two fixed human calibrators vote independently. Publication requires a clear audit and unanimous acceptance; one bounded revision is available. The contract never evaluates a learner, applicant, employee, or submission.
